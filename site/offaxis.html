  4. Tilt BS2 (both axes) to set the off-axis angle, or press “Set optimal off-axis angle”.In the FFT, the +1 order must sit clear of the centre (carrier ≥ 3 × object bandwidth) and inside the frame (carrier + bandwidth ≤ Nyquist).

A diagonal carrier (both axes) packs the orders best into the square Fourier plane. The irises are alignment tools: they are used before BS2 is tilted for the carrier.

How off-axis DHM works

The objective and tube lens image the sample onto the camera with magnification M. The reference beam reaches the camera at a small angle θ, so the hologram is the sample image written onto straight carrier fringes of period λ/θ.

In the Fourier plane this gives three separate parts: the centre (the two intensities), and the +1 and −1 orders at ± the carrier frequency. Each order is a copy of the sample's complex field, with radius NA/(λM). Cutting out the +1 order and moving it to the centre recovers amplitude and phase from a single image.

The phase is the optical path difference: φ = 2π·Δn·thickness/λ. For red blood cells this gives the cell shape, including the central dimple.
